## Competing publication pathways

**A. Bounded empirical measurement study:** Negative/inconclusive conservative predictor performance, sensitivity to questionable stronger-test labels, and exact-run reproducibility. This matches the currently frozen evidence, but journal novelty/impact is **unverified**.

**B. Semantic-defect prediction study:** Requires independent expert label adjudication, more independent positive tasks, and at least one task-disjoint or different-agent predictive replication. None is complete; claiming this from the present paper would overstate the evidence.

The scientific framing decision is best made with the mentor **before** purely cosmetic editing. Neither former synthetic numbers nor later runner-only AUROC should be presented as independently verified semantic-defect prediction.
