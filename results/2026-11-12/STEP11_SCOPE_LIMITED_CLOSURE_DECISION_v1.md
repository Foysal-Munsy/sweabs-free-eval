## What semantic validity is, and is not, established

- **4** `invalid_test` (assertion conflicts with relevant standard/issue or lacks a sound justification);

- **16** `unresolved` (original issue scope versus augmented assertion still contested).

All **14 original UNVERIFIED** remain UNVERIFIED in the original runner and in the frozen Step 7 policy, even though **nine** now have new backend-targeted coverage, **four** have new non-destructive merged tests, and **one** has reconstructed full patch integrity. New experiments are **append-only diagnostic evidence**, not replacement official SWE-ABS scored runs. Three samples from the same task ID must remain one task cluster for uncertainty estimation.

Independent human/maintainer semantic annotation of every disputed test was **not conducted** and cannot honestly be inferred from deterministic test reruns. Reproducing a strengthened test failure does not prove the assertion is required by the original issue. The [Step 6 review](STEP6_FAIL_VALIDITY_REVIEW.md) records competing interpretations of the remaining eight unresolved issue families.

## Formal research claim and outcome decision

**Freeze and retain the original analysis choices**, not whatever yields a favorable effect:

- **Primary**: the original Step 7 `primary_y`: 153 audited runner PASS, 9 documentary-review-supported provisional FAIL, 36 excluded; 162 runs but only 61 tasks and **five distinct positive task IDs**. [Step 10 corrected task-cluster AUROC](STEP10_AUROC_TASK_CLUSTER_v2.json) = **0.489** for duplicate terminal command rate with 95% task-cluster interval **0.270–0.706**. **Conclusion: insufficient stable evidence of useful discrimination.**
- **Prespecified sensitivities**: noninvalid 153/27/18 (duplicate AUROC **0.721**), runner-only 153/31/14 (**0.708**), and hypothetical skipped-as-PASS 162/9/27 (**0.478**). These are **descriptive, label-policy-sensitive in-sample associations**, not confirmation of semantic defect prediction. [Frozen Step 7 label policy](STEP7_TRIAGE_AND_LABEL_POLICY.md), [corrected Step 10 results](STEP10_AUROC_TASK_CLUSTER_v2.json), [posthoc Step11 task robustness](STEP11_EXPLORATORY_ROBUSTNESS_v1.json).
- **Do not** replace old labels with new PostgreSQL PASS or the controlled SymPy/Django failure results in primary. No after-the-fact feature threshold tuning or silent label changes. If future human-oracle data are obtained, release a **separately versioned new benchmark/analysis snapshot**, explicitly *post hoc*, and recompute statistics under declared assumptions.

**Permitted paper thesis:** *A reproducible, task-aware pilot finds that simple trajectory-level indicators do not robustly distinguish reviewed strengthened-test successes from documentary-supported failures in this small sample; broader runner-label associations are strongly sensitive to test-validity policy, with process measurement and external validity limitations.*

**Not permitted without new evidence:** "We detected unreliable coding-agent successes", "our detector reliably predicts actual semantic failures", "all strengthened tests are valid", "UNVERIFIED patches passed under official SWE-ABS", "we have a calibrated deployable classifier", "submission-ready journal proof", or any causal claim.
